    Ground yourself · right here

    One stone. One breath. Seven times.

    Before you book anything, take a minute. Place a stone on the cairn, then breathe with it: in for four, out for six. Seven stones, seven breaths, about a minute. There is no way to do this wrong. Prefer the sun or your senses? They are one tab over.

    Stone 0 of 7

    Tap the cairn to place your first stone.

    Breathe in for 4, hold for 4, out for 6. Three rounds is plenty. Unclench your jaw. Let your heels get heavy. You can also tap the scene to start or pause.

    Come back to your senses, one at a time. Go slowly, and let each answer land before the next. Five, four, three, two, one.

    1. 5things you can see: a colour, a shadow, the light on a wall.
    2. 4things you can feel: your feet in your socks, the chair beneath you.
    3. 3things you can hear: near, far, and the quiet between.
    4. 2things you can smell, or two scents you love.
    5. 1thing you can taste, then one slow breath out.

    Inside a session

    Four movements.

    Both practices are quiet and unhurried, and you never need to do anything but rest. Here's how the hour unfolds with me. Hover or focus a step to see it on the drawing.

    01

    Settle in

    Shoes off, blanket on. We'll chat for a few minutes about how you're feeling today and what you'd like from the session.

    02

    Light, still hands

    My hands rest lightly on, or just above, your body, head to feet, with no pressure or manipulation.

    03

    The drift

    Most people sink somewhere between awake and asleep. Warmth, tingling or simply deep quiet are all normal.

    04

    Lighter on your feet

    A slow return, a glass of water, and you leave at your booked time, grounded and unhurried.

    01

    Settle in

    You recline in a comfy chair, shoes and socks off, blanket on. We start with a short check-in about how you're feeling.

    02

    Warming the feet

    I gently warm up both feet, toes first, so they can soften and relax before any focused work.

    03

    Working the map

    Focused pressure moves through the reflex zones, toes to heel. Just tell me and I'll adjust the pressure to your comfort.

    04

    Lighter on your feet

    A slow return, a glass of water, and you leave at your booked time, feeling grounded.

    Questions

    Good to know.

    Anything I haven't answered here? Call or text me at 587-216-8588. I'm always happy to chat.

    How do I book, and why a deposit?
    Pick a time that suits you on my schedule, then pop in a small $40 deposit to hold it for you. It comes off your session total, and it's fully refundable up to 48 hours before your appointment. Within 48 hours I'm not able to refund it, because that time was set aside just for you.
    How do I cancel or move my booking?
    Text me at 587-216-8588 or send me an email at least 48 hours before your appointment, and I'll refund your deposit in full or find you a new time. Within 48 hours I'm not able to refund the deposit, because that time was held just for you.
    I have a package. Do I need to pay a deposit?
    No. Book with the same email you used to buy it, and I'll recognise your package, show how many sessions are left and ask if you'd like to use one, with nothing to pay. Packages are just for the person who bought them, and I find them by email, so it needs to be the same address. Packages don't expire, and they're non-refundable. If you need to cancel, text or email me at least 48 hours ahead and your session stays in your package. A no-show or a cancellation within 48 hours uses that session.
    How does booking work with a membership?
    Book with the same email as your membership and I'll recognise you, with nothing to pay at booking. Memberships are just for the member, and I find them by email, so it needs to be the same address. Your membership includes one session a month. If you need to cancel, text or email me at least 48 hours ahead so you can rebook within the month. A no-show or a cancellation within 48 hours uses that month's session.
    How do I cancel my membership?
    You can cancel anytime with 30 days’ notice. Just email me at hello@soleserenity.ca, or call or text 587-216-8588. Your membership continues for those 30 days, so you can still book your session. After that, bookings go back to the usual $40 deposit.
    Can I book with a gift card?
    Yes, and there's no deposit. Gift cards can be bought for anyone: book in the name the card was given to, or tap "Have a gift card code?" and enter the code. I'll take the session from your gift card. A no-show or a cancellation within 48 hours is also taken from your gift card, so please text or email me at least 48 hours ahead if plans change.
    Is there an offer for my first visit?
    Yes. If it's your first time with me, you can book two hours of Reflexology + Reiki (one full hour of each) for $99, half the usual $198. It's for new clients only, once per person. Choose "First visit · $99" when you book.
    Can I have some Reiki in my reflexology session?
    They're two separate sessions, so a reflexology hour is all reflexology. If you'd love both, book the 2-hour Combination. You'll have one full hour of each, back to back, for $198.
    Where are you located?
    I'm in Northwest Calgary. To keep my studio private, I send you the exact address once your deposit is in.
    What should I wear?
    Whatever feels comfortable. For reflexology your clothes stay on, and only your shoes and socks come off. For Reiki you stay fully clothed. Loose layers are perfect.
    What if I'm running late?
    Please don't stress. Your session still finishes at the booked time, so the next client's time is protected too. Arriving a few minutes early gives you the most time to relax.
    I have allergies or fragrance sensitivities.
    Just let me know when you book. I can make your session completely scent-free, and I only ever use essential oils with your consent.
    Will my insurance cover it?
    Your receipts include my RCRT registration (Reg. No. ST26-26049). Some extended health plans cover reflexology, so it's worth checking with your provider. For package and membership visits, each receipt shows that visit's share of what you paid (for example $91.67 for each session of a $275 package). For the Combination, the receipt covers the reflexology hour.
    Is this a replacement for medical care?
    No. Reflexology and Reiki are complementary practices that support your wellbeing. They never replace care from your doctor or other health professionals, so please keep up any treatment you're receiving.
